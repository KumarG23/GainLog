# Cardio rollout status

The Wednesday `recovery` plan identity is retained for history and remote-plan compatibility, but its built-in title is now **Intervals**. Loading it drafts a 10-minute warm-up, four 4-minute hard intervals with three 3-minute easy recoveries, and a 5-minute cool-down. First-week-back users can select three rounds; the easy-cardio alternative remains strength-free. Segment durations start blank: prescriptions are not logged as performed work. Enter actual minutes for each completed segment. Existing drafts are never silently replaced.

Readiness checks the last completed calendar night against the preceding seven calendar days. Sleep under six hours, HRV more than 15% below baseline, or resting HR at least five bpm above baseline offers easy zone 2 by default; the user can explicitly choose intervals. Missing history is **unknown**, not a veto and not a clear signal. The easy zone 2 band is an editable local estimate in Settings, not a measured threshold. Google-owned daily wearable fields remain owned by Google in the server's reconciled daily-health endpoint; Health Connect is fallback.

**Not yet implemented:** permissioned Health Connect heart-rate sample capture, sample-derived time-in-band and weekly minutes, interval HR summaries, monthly treadmill benchmark and its trend/coach comparisons. The current workout-level manually entered average HR must not be used for fitness comparisons or reported as sample-derived zone minutes. Do not present interval session counts as complete merely because a draft was opened or a warm-up was logged. No live workout-plan override or deployment is part of this change.
