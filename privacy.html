PRIVACY POLICY

GainLog privacy policy

Effective August 26, 2026

Scope

GainLog is a private, single-user fitness, nutrition, and health-trend application. This policy describes how GainLog handles information obtained through Google Health API authorization.

Data GainLog accesses

With the user's explicit consent, GainLog may request read-only access to selected Google Health sleep, activity and fitness, and health metrics and measurements data. This can include sleep sessions and stages, steps, distance, calories, exercise sessions, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability.

GainLog does not request Google Health write access.

How data is used

Authorized data is used only to present the user's private health and fitness history, calculate personal trends, support workout and recovery review, and generate user-requested coaching inside GainLog.

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Storage and security

GainLog stores imported health records in an owner-controlled private service. GainLog does not currently retain a Google Health refresh credential. When production authorization is enabled, OAuth credentials will be kept server-side, encrypted at rest, and excluded from the mobile application. Access to the service is restricted to the owner's private network.

Retention and deletion

Imported records are retained until the owner deletes them or retires the service. Google Health authorization can always be revoked through the user's Google Account connections page. When production connection controls are enabled, GainLog will also provide a disconnect action. Revocation stops future imports. The owner can delete imported records and stored OAuth credentials from the private GainLog service.

Limited Use

GainLog's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Public access and third parties

GainLog does not provide public account registration. It does not disclose Google Health data to third parties except when required to operate a user-requested feature under the owner's control or when legally required.

Changes

This policy may be updated when GainLog's data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or requests can be submitted through the GainLog support tracker.
